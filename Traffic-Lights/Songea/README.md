# 📖 Case Study Overview

This case study documents practical traffic light repair and maintenance work carried out in **Songea Town, Ruvuma Region**.

The work involved investigating an operational problem, inspecting the traffic signal system, performing appropriate tests, identifying the fault area, carrying out corrective action and verifying system operation after the repair.

The troubleshooting process followed a systematic engineering approach rather than replacing components based on assumptions.

---

# ⚠️ 1. Reported / Observed Problem

A fault was identified within the traffic signal system and required technical inspection and corrective maintenance.

The initial objective was to determine whether the problem was related to:

- Power supply
- Wiring or connections
- Signal heads
- Controller
- Protection devices
- Other related system components

> The exact fault is documented only where supported by actual field measurements and observations.

---

# 🔍 2. Initial Inspection

A visual and functional inspection was performed before component replacement.

The inspection focused on:

- Traffic signal heads
- Controller equipment
- Power supply
- Electrical connections
- Wiring
- Terminals and connectors
- Protection devices
- Visible physical damage
- Signs of overheating
- Signs of water ingress

The purpose was to identify possible fault areas before performing detailed testing.

---

# ⚡ 3. Electrical & System Testing

Appropriate tests were performed to help isolate the source of the fault.

The diagnostic process considered:

- Power supply condition
- Electrical connections
- Controller operation
- Signal head operation
- Wiring and terminals
- System response

Measurements and observations were considered together with the overall circuit/system condition.

> A component was not considered faulty based on a single abnormal reading alone. Additional checks were required to support the diagnosis.

---
